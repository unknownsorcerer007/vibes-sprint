Quick Q & A

Q: Do I need prior experience to win?

A: No experience required. Judging is entirely product-side: does it work, is it well-designed, is it accessible, is it useful. How you built it — and with what tools — is irrelevant to your score.

Q: What is the Spectator path?

A: Spectators join without building. During the 30-minute testing phase they test submitted products and earn a share of the pool for verified, quality reviews. Spectators can upgrade to builders in future competitions at any time.
